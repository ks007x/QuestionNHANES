```
SPECIFIC AIMS (seed - edit before use)

Gap. Nationally representative evidence on functional iron deficiency is limited, and cross-sectional estimates cannot establish temporality or mechanism.

Preliminary data. In a prespecified, survey-weighted analysis of 8,212 NHANES participants, we found 15.4% (95% CI, 14.4%-16.5%). The analysis is fully reproducible (locked protocol, provenance ledger, code bundle).

Aim 1. Replicate and extend the finding across additional NHANES cycles and subgroups (sex, race and ethnicity, age, income), with prespecified sensitivity analyses.
Aim 2. Test temporality in a longitudinal cohort with repeated measures of functional iron deficiency.
Aim 3. Evaluate mechanism or intervention targets suggested by the cross-sectional pattern.

Fit. Secondary-data aims suit R03/R21; Aims 2-3 suit a K-award or R01. Confirm institute priorities with a program officer.
```
