## Specific Aims seed

```
SPECIFIC AIMS (seed - edit before use)

Gap. Nationally representative evidence on abdominal obesity and glycemic control is limited, and cross-sectional estimates cannot establish temporality or mechanism.

Preliminary data. In a prespecified, survey-weighted analysis of 6,610 NHANES participants, we found Mean difference 0.15 (95% CI, 0.10-0.21). The analysis is fully reproducible (locked protocol, provenance ledger, code bundle).

Aim 1. Replicate and extend the finding across additional NHANES cycles and subgroups (sex, race and ethnicity, age, income), with prespecified sensitivity analyses.
Aim 2. Test temporality in a longitudinal cohort with repeated measures of abdominal obesity and glycemic control.
Aim 3. Evaluate mechanism or intervention targets suggested by the cross-sectional pattern.

Fit. Secondary-data aims suit R03/R21; Aims 2-3 suit a K-award or R01. Confirm institute priorities with a program officer.
```
