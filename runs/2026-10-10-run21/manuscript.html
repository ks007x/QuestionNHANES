Introduction

Whether toxocara seropositivity - IgG antibody positive (Luminex rTc-CTL-1, MFI >23.1) is associated with eosinophilia - absolute eosinophil count ≥0.5 x10^9/L beyond established risk factors is uncertain in nationally representative data. National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) data permit design-based estimation that generalizes to the noninstitutionalized US population. We aimed to evaluate whether toxocara seropositivity - IgG antibody positive (Luminex rTc-CTL-1, MFI >23.1) is associated with eosinophilia - absolute eosinophil count ≥0.5 x10^9/L among US adults.

Methods

Data Source

NHANES is a continuous, nationally representative survey of the US civilian noninstitutionalized population using a stratified, multistage probability design, with household interviews and standardized physical examinations and laboratory testing in mobile examination centers (MECs). We used NHANES 2011-2012. NHANES protocols were approved by the National Center for Health Statistics Research Ethics Review Board and participants provided written informed consent; this analysis of deidentified public-use data did not require additional institutional review board review. We followed the Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology (STROBE) reporting guideline.

Statistical Analysis

Binary outcome: survey-weighted quasi-Poisson regression with log link estimates prevalence ratios (PRs). PRs are preferred to odds ratios for cross-sectional data because ORs exaggerate associations when the outcome is common (>10%); the quasi-Poisson variance is robust under the design-based (Taylor linearization) estimator. Logistic regression is run as a sensitivity analysis. All analyses incorporated the WTSSTT2Y sample weight (subsample component), masked variance pseudo-strata and pseudo-PSUs (Taylor series linearization; 14 strata, 31 PSUs, design df = 17); subpopulations were analyzed with domain (subset) estimation to preserve the full design. Proportions use Korn-Graubard 95% CIs and were evaluated against the NCHS Data Presentation Standards for Proportions. Regression inference used t distributions with design degrees of freedom. Covariates were selected as plausible common causes of toxocara seropositivity - IgG antibody positive (Luminex rTc-CTL-1, MFI >23.1) and eosinophilia - absolute eosinophil count ≥0.5 x10^9/L (minimal confounder set from the domain library): Age, Sex, Race and ethnicity, Income-to-poverty ratio, Body mass index, and Born in the United States. Variables on the causal pathway or sharing laboratory components with the outcome/exposure are excluded to avoid over-adjustment. Effect modification was assessed with Wald F tests of product terms (Sex, Age group). Complete-case primary analysis within the design; multiple imputation by chained equations as sensitivity analysis when covariates are missing. Prespecified sensitivity analyses: Alternative outcome definition: AEC ≥1.5; Logistic regression (odds ratio); Multiple imputation of missing covariates (m = 10); Unweighted analysis (design ignored). E-values quantified the minimum strength of unmeasured confounding needed to explain away the primary association. Two-sided P < .05 denoted significance. Analyses used R version 4.6.1 (2026-06-24) with survey 4.5, mice 3.19.0 and ggplot2 4.0.3, orchestrated by Q2E.

Results

The analytic sample included 4,579 participants (weighted to represent approximately 232.0 million US residents; 51.6% were female (weighted) and 16.9% were aged 65 years or older). Overall, toxocara seropositivity - IgG antibody positive (Luminex rTc-CTL-1, MFI >23.1) was associated with Eosinophilia - absolute eosinophil count ≥0.5 x10^9/L (PR, 2.16 (95% CI, 1.36-3.42)) after adjustment for age, sex, race and ethnicity, income-to-poverty ratio, body mass index, born in the united states. 3 of 4 sensitivity analyses agree in direction (associations) or magnitude (prevalence). Divergent: Alternative outcome definition: AEC ≥1.5

The E-value for the primary estimate was 3.74 (CI limit 2.07); Prevalence ratio used directly.

Effect modification by sex: P = .74 for interaction (Male: 2.05 (1.16-3.63); Female: 2.23 (0.92-5.41)).

Effect modification by age group: P = .29 for interaction (18-39: 2.88 (1.42-5.86); 40-64: 2.12 (0.89-5.02); 65+: 1.72 (0.62-4.78)).

Discussion

In this nationally representative cross-sectional analysis, toxocara seropositivity - IgG antibody positive (Luminex rTc-CTL-1, MFI >23.1) was associated with Eosinophilia - absolute eosinophil count ≥0.5 x10^9/L (PR, 2.16 (95% CI, 1.36-3.42)) after adjustment for age, sex, race and ethnicity, income-to-poverty ratio, body mass index, born in the united states. Strengths include a prespecified, investigator-approved protocol; guideline-versioned, citable case definitions; design-based variance estimation with appropriate examination weights; NCHS reliability standards; multiple prespecified sensitivity analyses; and a fully reproducible, provenance-tracked pipeline. Limitations: Cross-sectional design: temporality cannot be established and associations must not be interpreted causally. Single measurement of laboratory values; within-person variability and chronicity cannot be assessed. Residual confounding by unmeasured or imperfectly measured factors (e.g., diet, medications, menstrual blood loss). Self-reported comorbidities and behaviours are subject to recall and social-desirability bias. NHANES excludes institutionalized and active-duty military populations. Operational definition (Toxocara IgG+): Surplus-specimen subsample (participants aged 6+ with stored sera): analysed with the surplus-specimen weights (WTSSTT2Y), not the full exam weights.

Limitations

  • NHANES excludes institutionalized and active-duty military populations.
  • Operational definition (Toxocara IgG+): Surplus-specimen subsample (participants aged 6+ with stored sera): analysed with the surplus-specimen weights (WTSSTT2Y), not the full exam weights.

  • Operational definition (Toxocara IgG+): 2013-2014 results exist but the current CDC release does not include their subsample weights; 2011-2012 is used.

Conclusions

In this cross-sectional study of US adults, toxocara seropositivity - IgG antibody positive (Luminex rTc-CTL-1, MFI >23.1) was associated with Eosinophilia - absolute eosinophil count ≥0.5 x10^9/L (PR, 2.16 (95% CI, 1.36-3.42)) after adjustment for age, sex, race and ethnicity, income-to-poverty ratio, body mass index, born in the united states. This association is cross-sectional and may reflect residual confounding or reverse causation; prospective studies are needed to establish temporality.
