# Five-minute demo script

0. **Answerability** (30 s). Type *"Is hearing loss associated with dementia in older adults?"* and click **Can NHANES answer this?** It reports that dementia isn't measured, points to the audiometry component, and suggests CFQ cognitive scores instead.
1. **Ask** (30 s). Paste the test question from the final day, or use the challenge example: *"I have noticed that adults with greater abdominal obesity may have worse blood sugar control even at similar weight."* Point out the data-boundary chip (offline agents, public data policy) and the cycle and data-source selectors.
2. **Protocol** (60 s). Click **Run blinded feasibility probe**: real counts per cycle, expected CI width and the minimum detectable effect, with no results revealed. In the agent roundtable, show that the Investigator read "at similar weight" as a reason to adjust for BMI and the Epidemiologist removed diabetes as over-adjustment. Change the exposure scale, or swap exposure and outcome, to show it's editable. Show the feasibility score.
3. **Data map** (30 s). Walk from waist circumference to `BMXWAIST` in `P_BMX`, and from HbA1c to `LBXGH` in `P_GHB`. Show availability by cycle and the dictionary explorer (search "transferrin").
4. **Plan** (60 s). Model family and its rationale, the auto-generated sensitivity analyses (binary HbA1c ≥6.5% PR, excluding anemia, MI, quartiles, alternative adiposity measures), and Reviewer items with one-click fixes. Approve: note that the approval is locked to the plan hash.
5. **Run & validate** (45 s). Live R log, then the QC grid (weights, design df, NCHS standards, VIF for waist plus BMI), the participant flow and the source-file MD5s.
6. **Results** (45 s). Headline mean difference with E-value, forest plot across adjustment sets, sensitivity analyses and strata, Table 1 by ATP III abdominal obesity, the plain-language summary read aloud via ElevenLabs, PubMed prior literature, and the JAMA-style manuscript.
7. **Sign-off** (30 s). Attestations, an override reason for any failed check, the certificate with plan and results hashes, and the hash-chained ledger.
8. **Bonus**. Switch to *"Do a research study on the prevalence of functional iron deficiency"* and show the replication card against Tawfik et al. 2024. Open the Definitions library to show ASH 2026, KDIGO 2026 and Baveno VIII with operational notes, plus Guideline watch.
